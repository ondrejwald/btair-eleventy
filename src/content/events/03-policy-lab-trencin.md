What stays in a place after an artistic residency ends? And what do the people who welcome artists, build local connections and keep residency spaces running need?

Join the BtAIR Policy Lab in Trenčín to share experiences and explore better conditions for artistic residencies — how they can support artists, hosts and local communities, and how practical experience can inform cultural policy. We invite artists, residency hosts, cultural and community organisations and public administration representatives. **You do not need to know BtAIR or be a policy expert** — your experience is what matters.

In the first hour, we will introduce BtAIR, its residency models, the btair.eu platform and a manual for preparing open calls. The second hour will focus on discussion and concrete recommendations.
