How can artistic residencies give artists time to explore, connect with local communities and support the people who make them possible? Bridging the A-I-R will be presented by **Davide D'Antonio**, Director of Wonderland Festival, on 20 November at 7 pm — as part of a festival edition with a special focus on Czech performing arts.

Organised by IDRA Teatro, a BtAIR project partner, Wonderland brings together artists, audiences and cultural professionals across theatre, dance, music and other art forms. The presentation will introduce BtAIR's approach to residencies — care, sustainability and freedom to explore — covering the ten residency models, pilot exchanges, the role of local cultural mediators, and the btair.eu and national ART-IN-RES platforms.

_Festival runs 6–29 November 2026. Presentation date confirmed; exact time to be confirmed._
