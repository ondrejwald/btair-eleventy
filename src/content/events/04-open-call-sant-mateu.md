Nová síť and Inestable invite Czech-based performing and live arts creators to apply for a fully funded 14-day residency at Projecte Somiatruites in Sant Mateu, Spain (3–16 May 2027).

This residency offers time, space and support to explore your artistic practice through dialogue and shared creation with the local rural community. Supported practices include **dance, theatre, performance and interdisciplinary approaches**.

The grant covers a €1,000 artist fee per person, €40 daily subsistence, full travel costs, accommodation, versatile working spaces, family / caregiver support, and full-time on-site translation and mediation. Up to 5 artists total.

Apply by **11 January 2027** via Eventival.

_More info and application: [artinres.cz/en/open-calls/bridging-the-a-i-r-2027](https://artinres.cz/en/open-calls/bridging-the-a-i-r-2027/)_
