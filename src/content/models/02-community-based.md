---
title: "Community-Based"
code: "M2"
order: 2
---
Treats the residency as a space for co-creation with local people and places. Artists work in dialogue with the community through open exchange, voluntary participation and shared experience. Local knowledge and relationships become creative resources. For artists, groups and collectives in rural, peripheral or urban settings — typically 10 to 14 days, with reciprocal benefits that continue after the residency ends.
