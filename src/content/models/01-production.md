Supports professional artistic creation across all stages of the process, from research to final presentation. Instead of pushing artists toward a finished piece, the model creates optimal conditions for experimentation, development and public sharing. Formats remain flexible — works in progress, open studios, performances or workshops. For individual artists, groups and companies working in theatre, dance, circus, performance and interdisciplinary practices.
