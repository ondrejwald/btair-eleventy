Brings artists together with professionals from other fields — education, sciences, ecology, technology — to open new ways of thinking, researching and creating. Infrastructure gives access to specialised resources, equipment and expertise beyond the arts. For individuals, groups and established companies ready for professional-level collaboration across disciplines. Typically 7 to 20 days, with outcomes ranging from research and prototypes to performances and public presentations.
