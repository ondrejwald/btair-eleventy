Strengthens the people, teams and organisations that make artistic work possible. Instead of focusing on an artistic output, this residency gives organisations time and space to reflect, plan and learn together — through coaching, peer learning, job shadowing and strategic planning. For artistic directors, producers, cultural managers and whole teams. Short format (2 to 10 days), with outcomes such as action plans, strategic roadmaps and new working methods.
