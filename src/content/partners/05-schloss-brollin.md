schloss bröllin is an international centre for performing arts and artistic residencies in a historic estate in north-eastern Germany. It supports artistic processes through long-term residencies, workshops and professional meetings. schloss bröllin contributes residency hosting and practical expertise in sustainable mobility to Bridging the A-I-R.
