Nová síť is a Czech service and advisory organisation for live arts and open culture. It connects artists, cultural organisations and public bodies, develops residency programmes and supports the independent cultural sector through advocacy and professional training. Nová síť leads Bridging the A-I-R and coordinates its communication, research and public sharing of results.
