IDRA Teatro is a performing arts organisation based in Brescia, Italy, working with contemporary creation and artistic development. It programmes performances, runs residency activities and connects artists with local audiences and professional partners. IDRA leads networking and capacity-building activities in Bridging the A-I-R.
