Anténa is a Slovak network of independent cultural centres and organisations. It connects cultural initiatives, supports exchange between regions and strengthens the position of independent culture in Slovak public life. Anténa coordinates the Slovak ART-IN-RES platform and contributes training activities to Bridging the A-I-R.
